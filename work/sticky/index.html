01 / Introduction

STICKY is an experimental music video that brings together original electronic music, AI-generated imagery, and visual storytelling.

The project explores the relationship between human creativity and artificial intelligence, treating AI not simply as a production tool, but as a creative partner.

02 / Creative Approach

Between control and accident.

Working with generative AI introduces an element of unpredictability into the creative process.

Instead of controlling every visual detail, I explored how unexpected results could become part of the artistic direction.

Through music, image generation, and editing, STICKY investigates the balance between creative intention and machine-generated possibilities.
